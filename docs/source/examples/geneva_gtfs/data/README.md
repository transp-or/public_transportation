# Committed Geneva scenario data

The files in this directory are generated by `../tools/extract_geneva_gtfs.py`.
Do not hand-edit them; modify the extraction rules and regenerate instead.

- `stops.csv`: 62 physical stops obtained by consolidating GTFS platforms.
- `lines.csv`: TPG tram lines 12, 14, and 18.
- `trips.csv`: the 173 trips intersecting 07:00–09:00, with original GTFS trip IDs.
- `stop_times.csv`: complete ordered stop sequences for those trips.
- `time_bins.csv`: four half-hour OD departure intervals.
- `true_demand.csv`: synthetic truth over the complete OD universe.
- `prior_demand.csv`: deliberately inaccurate positive baselines for free cells.
- `fixed_demand.csv`: all structurally inactive OD/time cells, fixed at zero.
- `metadata.json`: scenario metadata consumed by the package.
- `provenance.json`: machine-readable source and extraction audit record.

The timetable and stop coordinates are derived from the Swiss national GTFS;
the OD matrices are synthetic and are not observations of Geneva passengers.

Source attribution: **opentransportdata.swiss**.
