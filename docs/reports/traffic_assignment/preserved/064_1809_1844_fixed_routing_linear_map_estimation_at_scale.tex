\subsubsection{Fixed-routing linear MAP estimation at scale}
\label{sec:fixed-routing-linear-map}

When $\theta$ and all generalized costs are fixed, routing probabilities are
constant and the complete measurement prediction is affine in the free OD
demand.  Let $\bm f_{\mathcal U}\in\mathbb R^n$ denote the free physical flows,
let $m$ be the number of observations, and let $\bm c\in\mathbb R^m$ be the
measurement contribution of strictly positive frozen demand.  The fixed-routing
model is
\[
\widehat{\bm y}=A\bm f_{\mathcal U}+\bm c,
\qquad A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}.
\]
Frozen-zero cells have no column in $A$, while positive frozen cells contribute
only to $\bm c$.  Thus neither kind of frozen cell increases the estimation
dimension.

The implemented linear MAP problem uses positive observation weights
$W=\operatorname{diag}(\bm w)$, componentwise physical bounds
$\bm\ell\leq\bm f_{\mathcal U}\leq\bm u$, and an explicitly selected ordered
collection of linear regularization blocks $(L_k,\bm r_k,\lambda_k)$:
\[
\min_{\bm\ell\leq\bm f_{\mathcal U}\leq\bm u}
\left\{
\frac12\left\|W^{1/2}
  (A\bm f_{\mathcal U}+\bm c-\bm y)\right\|_2^2
+\frac12\sum_k\lambda_k
  \left\|L_k\bm f_{\mathcal U}-\bm r_k\right\|_2^2
\right\}.
\]
The supported choices are no regularization, ridge toward the prior demand, and
scaled ridge toward the prior demand.  No recommendation is applied implicitly.
This weighted least-squares mode is distinct from the negative-binomial ML and
MAP objectives described below: the two are comparable only when their
observation models and priors have deliberately been made equivalent.
