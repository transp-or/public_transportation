\subsubsection{Separation of routing preparation and demand loading}

For a destination group $g$, let $d_g$ denote its destination, let
$\mathcal A_g^+$ denote its enabled links after destination gating, and let
$\bm f_g$ collect the OD/time-bin demand injected for that group. The backward
value recursion and the resulting transition probabilities depend on
$(\mathcal G,\bm c,d_g,\mathcal A_g^+,\theta)$, but not on $\bm f_g$. The
assignment can therefore be written as two distinct operations:
\[
\mathcal R_g(\mathcal G,\bm c,d_g,\mathcal A_g^+,\theta)
= (\bm V_g,\bm P_g),
\]
followed by
\[
\bm x_g
=\mathcal L_g(\mathcal G,\bm P_g,\mathcal A_g^+,\bm f_g),
\qquad
\bm x=\sum_g \bm x_g,
\]
where $\mathcal R_g$ is routing preparation and $\mathcal L_g$ is the forward
flow-loading operation. For fixed probabilities, $\mathcal L_g$ is linear in
the injected demand. This separation is exact: it does not approximate the
route-choice model or alter its probabilities.
